How the Webflow connection works

1
Connect once
Paste your Webflow API token and Forge AI connects to your entire Webflow account instantly — all your sites, all your pages.
2
Forge AI reads your site data
Forge AI reads your page titles, meta descriptions, headings, images, and content directly from Webflow — no URL scanning needed.
3
Issues are found and fixed
Missing meta description? Forge AI writes one and pushes it back to Webflow automatically. Image missing alt text? Fixed. No manual work needed.
4
Daily monitoring
Every day Forge AI checks all your Webflow sites and alerts you if anything degrades — scores drop, new issues appear, or pages break.
