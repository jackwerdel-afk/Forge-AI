01
What Forge AI Is
Forge AI is a website intelligence platform built by Werdel Global Systems that scans and monitors client websites for SEO, performance, mobile, UX, and maintenance issues. We provide AI-powered analysis, recommendations, and automated fixes. For connected WordPress sites, Forge AI can automatically fix issues like missing meta descriptions, page titles, and image alt text using AI. For all platforms, we provide daily automated scanning, email alerts when scores drop, and white label reports for your clients. We provide analysis and recommendations — we do not guarantee specific results or search engine rankings.
02

03
What You Can Do
You may use Forge AI to monitor your own websites or your clients' websites. You may generate white label reports branded with your agency name. You may connect platforms — WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace — that you own or have explicit permission to connect on behalf of a client.
04
What You Cannot Do
You may not share your account with others or allow unauthorized access. You may not use Forge AI to scan websites you do not own or do not have permission to scan. You can disable auto-fix at any time from your dashboard.

Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026 · We keep this simple and honest.
01
What We Collect
When you create an account and use Forge AI we collect the following information:

• Your email address and agency name
• Website URLs you add to your account
• Scan results and scores for those websites
• Your notification and settings preferences
• Basic usage data such as when you log in and run scans
02
How We Use It
We use your data exclusively to provide the Forge AI service — to run scans, send you email alerts when site scores drop, store your results, and improve the product over time. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running Forge AI.
03
How We Store It
All data is stored securely in Supabase with row-level security enabled. This means your data is completely isolated from other users — no other Forge AI user can read or access your sites, scan results, or account information.
04
Third Party Services
To provide Forge AI we use a small number of trusted third party services. We only share what is strictly necessary for each service to function.
Supabase
Database and authentication. Stores your account data, site data, and scan results securely.
Anthropic (Claude AI)
Powers the AI scanning engine and auto-fix recommendations. Website content is sent to Claude for analysis. No personal user data is shared.
Google PageSpeed API
Used to measure website performance metrics like LCP, CLS, and TBT. Only the website URL is shared.
Resend
Used to send email alerts when a site's score drops. Only your email address is shared.
Stripe
Handles payment processing for paid plans. We never store your card details — Stripe handles all payment data.
05
Auto-Fix Data
When auto-fix is enabled on a WordPress site, Forge AI reads and writes page content including meta tags, titles, and image attributes via the Forge AI WordPress plugin. This content is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to generate appropriate fixes. No personal user data from your client sites is stored beyond what is needed to perform and log the fix.
06
What We Don't Do
We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers. We never use your client website data to train AI models. We never access your account without your permission.
07
Cookies
We use session cookies for authentication only — to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third party analytics cookies.
08
Your Rights
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings page. If you need help with a full data export or deletion request email us at privacy@forgeai-wgs.com and we will respond within 5 business days.
09
Children
Forge AI is not intended for anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account please contact us at privacy@forgeai-wgs.com and we will delete the account immediately.
10
Changes to This Policy
If we make material changes to this privacy policy we will notify you by email at least 14 days before they take effect.
