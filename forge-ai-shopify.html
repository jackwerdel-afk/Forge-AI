Enable Analytics on your Shopify stores

Add the Forge AI tracking snippet to your Shopify theme to enable visitor analytics. Paste it once in theme.liquid and analytics starts on every page automatically.

How to add in Shopify
1.
Open Theme Editor — In Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes. Click the three dots (...) next to your active theme and select Edit code.
2.
Open theme.liquid — In the Layout folder, click theme.liquid — this is the global file that loads on every page.
3.
Paste before — Find the closing </head> tag and paste the snippet on a new line just above it.
4.
Save — Click Save — the tracker is now live on your entire Shopify store.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use my custom domain or .myshopify.com? ▼
Use your custom domain if you have one — that is what customers see and what matters for SEO. If you have not connected a custom domain yet, the .myshopify.com URL works fine for monitoring.
Can I monitor multiple Shopify stores? ▼
Yes. Add each store separately by URL. Each gets its own dashboard card with individual daily scans and score alerts.
Will Forge AI affect my store performance? ▼
No. Forge AI is a passive scanner — it reads pages the same way a regular visitor would. The analytics snippet is tiny and loads asynchronously, so it has no impact on store speed.
When will I get score alerts? ▼
Forge AI scans every morning and alerts you only if a score drops 10 or more points since the last scan.
Does this work on Shopify Plus? ▼
Yes. Forge AI works with all Shopify plans. The theme.liquid approach for the analytics snippet works on all plans.
