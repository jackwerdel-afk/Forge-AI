Enable Analytics on your Weebly sites

Add the Forge AI tracking snippet to enable visitor analytics. Paste it once in Weebly settings and analytics starts automatically on every page.

How to add in Weebly
1.
Open Weebly Editor — Log into your Weebly account and open the site in the editor.
2.
Go to Settings → SEO — Click Settings in the top navigation bar, then click SEO in the left sidebar.
3.
Paste in Header Code — Scroll down to find the Header Code field and paste the Forge AI tracking snippet there — this adds it to every page automatically.
4.
Save and Publish — Click Save at the bottom, then click Publish in the top right for the changes to take effect.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work with custom Weebly domains? ▼
Yes. Use your custom domain if you have one — that is what matters for SEO. Otherwise the .weebly.com URL works fine for monitoring.
What if the site is not published yet? ▼
Forge AI can only monitor publicly accessible sites. The site must be published and live for monitoring to work.
Can I monitor multiple Weebly sites? ▼
Yes. Add each site separately by URL. Each gets its own dashboard card with individual daily scans and alerts.
When will I get score alerts? ▼
Forge AI scans every morning and alerts you only if a score drops 10 or more points since the last scan.
Where exactly is the Header Code field in Weebly? ▼
Go to Settings → SEO and scroll to the bottom of the page. The Header Code field is in the Search Engine Optimization section.
