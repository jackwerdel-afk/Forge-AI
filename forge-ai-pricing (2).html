Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account settings. You keep access until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees.
What counts as a client site?
Each unique domain counts as one site. Subdomains of the same domain count as one site.
Does it work with any website platform?
Yes. Forge AI works with WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace, and any custom HTML site. Full real-time alerts on WordPress and Webflow.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.
Who sees the reports — me or my clients?
You see everything in your Forge AI dashboard. Your clients only see what you choose to share — white label reports with your agency branding.
Is there a free trial?
Yes — your first 3 scans are completely free. No credit card required to try it out.
