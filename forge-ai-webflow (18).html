Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Connect with Webflow and Add by URL? ▼
Connect with Webflow uses OAuth — you authorize once and Forge AI automatically discovers every site on your Webflow account. Add by URL lets you monitor any single site without authorizing your full account.
Is the OAuth connection secure? ▼
Yes. The OAuth flow uses Webflow official authorization. Forge AI only requests read access to your site data — it cannot make changes to your Webflow sites. You can revoke access at any time from your Webflow dashboard.
Can I add more Webflow sites later? ▼
Yes. Come back to this page anytime and go through the Connect with Webflow flow again — new sites will appear in the checklist. You can also add individual sites by URL at any time.
When will I get alerts? ▼
Forge AI scans your Webflow sites every morning and alerts you only if a score drops 10 or more points since the last scan.
Does this work with Webflow Ecommerce sites? ▼
Yes. Forge AI monitors the publicly accessible pages of any Webflow site including Ecommerce stores. It checks SEO health, performance, SSL, and uptime regardless of site type.
